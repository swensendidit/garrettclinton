welcome 👋

I'm an investor at Westbound Equity PartnersInvestor, Director of Super AngelsEarly-stage investing alongside entrepreneurs, professional athletes, and first-generation wealth creators., where I also direct our Super Angels program, and an MBA student at Stanford GSB.

At the GSB I'm co-president of the VC Club and managing partner of the 27 Fund, our class's venture fund. I co-chair the Black Business Student Association, which hosts the Black Leadership Conference each February, and co-host Full Stack, a podcast on how founders and investors think about technology and capital.

Before Stanford I spent five years at Makena CapitalSenior Associate, Portfolio StrategyInstitutional portfolio strategy and outsourced CIO relationships for endowments, foundations, and families., managing portfolios for endowment and foundation clients and leading the firm's work with emerging venture managers. This past summer I was at X, the Moonshot FactoryBusiness Innovation ResidentSupported the capitalization of the lab's spin-outs..

I write about first-generation wealth: founders, early employees, and athletes who reach significant wealth before they have a process to manage it, and what it takes to get from a liquidity event to investing like an institution. Lately I've been thinking about how AI can give a small team the depth of a single-family office, inside a relationship a family already trusts. My newest piece is interactive.
